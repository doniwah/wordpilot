# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful environment for everyone participating in the WordPilot community.

We value contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives.

We pledge to make participation in our community a positive and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different perspectives and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors.
- Supporting newcomers and helping them learn.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Any other behavior considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all WordPilot community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories.
- Issues and Pull Requests.
- Discussions.
- Community communication channels.
- Official project events and activities.

It also applies when an individual officially represents the WordPilot project.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards.

Maintainers may take appropriate corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the severity and circumstances, maintainers may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Request corrective action.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Remove inappropriate content.
5. Permanently ban individuals from project spaces.

Maintainers reserve the right to determine appropriate consequences.
